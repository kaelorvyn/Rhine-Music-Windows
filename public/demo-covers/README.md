# Empty-library demonstration artwork

Three abstract images used only by the explicitly selected empty-library
demonstration, shown before the user has added any real music. Actual albums
always use their own local covers.

- `square.png`: warm sand, a low orange sun, quiet minimal geometric landscape.
- `portrait.png`: deep midnight blue, a small crescent moon above a still sea.
- `landscape.png`: cool mist and layered mountains.

The images are copied without cropping or stretching. `containCover` preserves
their original aspect ratio on every 3D card and in the independent viewer.
No album names or artist identities are invented for the real library.
